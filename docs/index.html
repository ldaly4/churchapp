For parishioners

Your parish.
Always close.

Neri is entirely personalised to your parish. Open it to find the next Mass, spend a moment in prayer or discover something to be part of.

  • A little space for faith

    Light a candle, pray a novena or follow along at Mass.

  • A way to take part

    Read the noticeboard, help in the parish garden or suggest a get-together.

  • A little easier to arrange

    Explore the sacraments, make an enquiry or support your parish.

Go on, have a look.

Tap and scroll inside the phone. Try Pray → Light a Candle, then press and hold a candle to light it.

This is a prototype for the Church of the Assumption, Delvin. Mass times, news and events are examples. Requests and donations are simulated. Please use made-up details when trying the forms.

For parish secretaries and administrators

Less admin.
More parish life.

A baptism enquiry here. A Mass intention there. A bulletin to finish before the phone rings again. Neri is being built to give your team a clearer view of what needs doing.

  • Keep bookings and enquiries in one place
  • Review and organise Mass intentions
  • Share parish news without typing it twice
  • See interest in groups, events and volunteering
  • Keep giving and donor authorisation status together
