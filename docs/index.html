Parish life is more than Sunday.

The choir, the coffee morning, the St Vincent de Paul, the youth group, the families preparing for Communion, the people abroad who still call it home. A parish is a community, and today it's held together by a newsletter, a noticeboard and a few phone numbers. Neri gives it one place to gather.

  • For the faithful: news, events and prayer in their pocket, and a simple way to get involved.
  • For parish groups and volunteers: rotas, sign-ups and messages without the endless group chats.
  • For the parish office: bookings, notices and giving in one place, posted once.

Everything your parish runs, in one place.

  • Groups and ministries

    Choir, SVP, youth, prayer groups, each with its own page and members.

  • Events and sign-ups

    Coffee mornings, pilgrimages, clean-ups, with numbers at a glance.

  • Volunteer rotas

    Readers, ministers of the Eucharist, collectors, flowers.

  • News and notices

    Post once, and it reaches phones, the website and the noticeboard.

  • Giving and fundraising

    Weekly offerings, appeals, the parish draw, and tax relief on gifts.

  • Bookings

    Sacraments, Mass intentions and the parish hall.

  • Prayer

    The daily Gospel, novenas and candles, for those near and far.

For the faithful

Their own parish in their pocket, with your church, your priest and your people.

  • See what's on and sign up in a tap
  • Join a group or volunteer
  • Read parish news and notices
  • Light a candle or pray a novena
  • Give to the parish from wherever they are

For the parish office

The parish office juggles calls, emails, the diary and the newsletter. Neri brings it into one place, so your team spends less time chasing information.

  • Manage bookings and parish enquiries
  • Review and schedule Mass intentions
  • Post news and notices once
  • Keep in touch with groups and volunteers
  • Organise giving and donor authorisations
