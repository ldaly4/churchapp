A parish is its people. Neri helps them find each other again.

Neri gives your parish one place where people can see what is happening, join in, pray together and lend a hand. Not just on Sunday, but all through the week.

Example: 43 families walking the Advent journey together at St Brigid's. One activity a week at home, one gathering in the church.

Everything your parish runs, in one place.

  • Groups and ministries

    Choir, SVP, youth, prayer groups, each with its own page and members.

  • Events and sign-ups

    Coffee mornings, pilgrimages, clean-ups, with numbers at a glance.

  • Volunteer rotas

    Readers, ministers of the Eucharist, collectors, flowers.

  • News and notices

    Post once, and it reaches phones, the website and the noticeboard.

  • Giving and fundraising

    Weekly offerings, appeals, the parish draw, and tax relief on gifts.

  • Bookings

    Sacraments, Mass intentions and the parish hall.

  • Prayer

    The daily Gospel, novenas and candles, for those near and far.

For the faithful

Their own parish in their pocket, with simple ways to meet, pray and take part.

  • Bring the community together: meet-ups, groups and volunteering, with simple sign-up.
  • Reach people who don't come every week: young adults and families find the parish where they already are, on their phones.
  • Pray together, even from away: keep emigrants and families abroad close to their home parish.
  • Support the parish: easy online giving, Mass cards and offerings, plus help with tax relief.

Ready-made ways for people to take part

Your parish chooses what to run, Neri provides the rest: the invitation, the sign-up, the reminders and the content.

Journeys and trails

Advent and Lent journeys families follow at home, then bring back to the church. A children's trail that turns a visit into an adventure.

Groups and meet-ups

Young adults, parents and toddlers, walking groups. Simple sign-up, so nobody has to wonder if they'll be the only one there.

Ways to help

The crib, the flowers, the garden, the tea after Mass. Clear call-outs so newcomers can find their place.

Reach the people who don't come every week. Young adults, families who come for the big days, and people living away. Neri gives each of them a reason to stay connected between Sundays.

Less admin. More parish life.

The parish office juggles calls, emails, the diary and the newsletter. Neri brings it into one place, so your team spends less time chasing information.

  • Manage bookings and parish enquiries
  • Review and schedule Mass intentions
  • Post news and notices once
  • Keep in touch with groups and volunteers
  • Organise giving and donor authorisations
