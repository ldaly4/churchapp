Why we're building Neri

A parish is made
of people.

❧

Someone puts the kettle on. Someone unlocks the church. Someone remembers an anniversary, welcomes a new neighbour or asks, “Will we see you on Sunday?”

These small things are the life of a parish. Neri is being built to help them happen more often.

One place to find Mass times, hear a familiar voice, lend a hand or say a quiet prayer. And a simpler way for the parish office to keep it all moving.
