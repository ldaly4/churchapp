Saint of the day

27 October

St Odhrán of Iona

Odhrán was an Irish monk who sailed with St Colmcille to Iona in 563. Tradition says he was the first of the community to be buried there, and the old burial ground on the island, Reilig Odhráin, still carries his name. He is a patron of the Diocese of Waterford and Lismore.

Our patron · Feast day 15 August

The Assumption of Mary

At the end of her life on earth, Mary was taken up body and soul into heaven. Her feast on 15 August is a holy day of obligation in Ireland, and for generations it was a day of pilgrimage, prayer at holy wells and gathering as a community.
